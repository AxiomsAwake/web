AxiomsAwake Media Publisher

A private local tool for the AxiomsAwake operator to upload selected AxiomsAwake videos to YouTube.

What the tool does

The operator selects a local video file, its title, description, credits, visibility and playlist. The tool verifies that the authorized YouTube channel matches the selected AxiomsAwake channel before storing authorization or publishing. It checks existing uploads and playlists to support resuming and reconciling a selected publication.

Google authorization lets this local tool act on the operator’s YouTube channel. The tool is intended for the operator’s own authorized use. It is not a public upload service.

About this website

These pages provide information about the tool and its use of Google data. The publisher runs locally; this website does not accept uploads or account sign-ins.

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use before authorizing the tool.

Contact

For questions about the tool or these pages, open an issue in AxiomsAwake/web. Do not include credentials, authorization links or private media in a public issue.
