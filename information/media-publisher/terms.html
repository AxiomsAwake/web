Terms of Use

These terms describe the intended use of the private local AxiomsAwake Media Publisher.

Authorized operation

The publisher is intended for the AxiomsAwake operator to publish selected AxiomsAwake media to the operator’s authorized YouTube channel. Use it only with an account and channel you are authorized to manage, and with media and metadata you are authorized to upload.

The operator chooses the video, metadata, visibility and playlist and is responsible for reviewing those choices. The tool verifies the selected channel and keeps local publication progress to support resuming or reconciling an interrupted operation. This website supplies information about the tool; it does not provide a public uploader or account service.

Google and YouTube

Use of YouTube and Google services is subject to the applicable YouTube Terms of Service and Google Terms of Service. The publisher uses the YouTube API Services; the YouTube API Services Terms of Service apply to that integration.

See the Privacy Policy for requested access, local storage and ways to revoke authorization or manage uploaded content.

Contact

For questions about the tool or these pages, open an issue in AxiomsAwake/web. Do not include credentials, authorization links or private media in a public issue.
