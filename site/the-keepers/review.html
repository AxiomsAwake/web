The Keepers 0.10.0-demo.21 — Mineral Foundry

New Game opens the coastal Mineral Foundry campaign. Capture its relays, protect your command core and bring surviving funds into the next sector. The browser and native Player use the same battlefield and controls.

What to try

  • Watch a hit land. Follow a Scout shot or a Guardian round to its point of contact. A glancing strike, a direct hit and a ruptured machine have different motion, light and sound. Zoom in to see which armour or weapon part broke free. A lost gun can leave a surviving unit unable to shoot.
  • Watch the units beside a wreck. Remaining ammunition sets the size of a secondary blast. A nearby Scout can take damage and move. A spent machine leaves a quieter structural collapse. Craters and charred terrain survive a Save and reload.
  • Keep command of the field. Order arrows and selection marks follow moving units and the terrain under the cursor. Assign an officer to cross-group support, withdraw a damaged group and pay for replacements.
  • Continue your campaign. Win on network pressure to open Next Sector with the nanomass you earned. After a defeat, inspect Retake for the surviving force and funds. Save, reopen or branch your recording from the same Player.

Browser performance

On an RTX 2080 SUPER, Chromium 151 at 1280×800 recorded a 16.7 ms median frame interval over 34.25 seconds of active play. The 95th percentile was 33.3 ms, with one stall of 867 ms. Sustained 60 FPS, physical-device checks and human playtesting remain open for stable 0.10.0.
