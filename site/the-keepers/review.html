The Keepers 0.10.0-demo.22 — Mineral Foundry

Launch the coastal Mineral Foundry campaign. Capture its relays, protect your command core and take your remaining nanomass into the next sector.

What to try

  • Watch a Guardian take a hit. Armour can detach, a broken gun can stop firing, and a destroyed machine can push nearby units when its ammunition ignites. A full ammunition bank cuts a wider and deeper crater than a spent machine.
  • Save after a blast and reload. The terrain keeps its shape and ash. A deep crater can close a route that a shallower crater leaves open.
  • Win by holding the relays and take Next Sector from the result screen. Use the same menu to manage saves and recordings.

Browser performance

The Player 0.23.34 hardware candidate averaged 53 rendered frames per second across four seconds of active play after a cold resume on Chromium 151 with an RTX 2080 SUPER. Longer stalls are still visible. Sustained 60 FPS and human playtesting remain open for stable 0.10.0.
