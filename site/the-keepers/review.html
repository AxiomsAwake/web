The Keepers 0.10.0-demo.19 — Mineral Foundry campaign preview

This internal tester Web preview brings one seeded Mineral Foundry campaign, with Player 0.23.30. The same campaign also runs in the native Player.

What to try

  • Start a campaign. New Game opens a fresh coastal basin. Capture Orange relays and follow the objective's remaining capture count to break network pressure.
  • Command and recover your force. Give groups distinct orders, switch an officer to cross-group support, withdraw damaged units, and pay for replacement Scouts to rejoin the battle.
  • Watch the battlefield change. Impacts damage individual machine parts. Pieces fractured from detached parts carry flames sized to each fragment; the flames shrink and fade as the pieces cool. Explosions leave craters that affect movement. If a crater blocks a group's route, it holds and retries while other units continue.
  • Continue after the Result. A network defeat can offer Retake on the same battlefield with surviving units and funds. Victory offers Next Sector, carrying remaining nanomass and the prior Result into a different basin.
  • Return to your campaign. Save a Retake or later sector, reopen it, and inspect the prior Result in Battle details. Try Recording seek, Return, and branch.

Preview limits

Packaged Linux and Firefox checks resumed an active battle, earned its network victory, and entered the next basin with the exact remaining funds. Continuous ordinary campaign play, the complete support and paid-rejoin journey, responsiveness, physical-device performance, accessibility, audio, and human acceptance remain under review. This preview does not establish stable 0.10.0 acceptance. Preserve older saves when testing a new build.
