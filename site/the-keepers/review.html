The Keepers 0.10.0-demo.13 — battle effects and straight movement

This tester build makes the reconstructed battle readable and physical: shots, explosions and wrecks now land on uneven ground, and units drive straight to their orders. Open the review and launch page for the exact build and game link. It is a development demo, not stable 0.10.0.

What to try

  • Watch a fight. Begin the ordinary battle and follow the first contact. Shots leave a muzzle flash and a tracer streak, hits throw sparks and scorch the ground, and destroyed machines shake the camera and burst into fire, sparks, smoke and tumbling plates, rods and wheels that bounce and settle. A destroyed core makes a much larger blast. Scorch marks fade after a while.
  • Pause mid-explosion. Effects freeze with the battle and continue on Resume, so you can inspect a moment without the presentation running ahead.
  • Order a move. Units now take a few straight legs to the destination instead of a staircase of right-angle steps, and still go around blocking terrain. Report any unit that hesitates, zigzags or cuts through an obstacle.
  • Look at the ground. The battlefield now has gentle visible relief instead of a flat plane. Report places where units or effects look detached from it.
  • Use the session menu. Session actions such as Reset Plan now close the menu when they act, and dialogs keep keyboard focus until they finish.
  • Recover a depleted group. Select a group that lost members and use the deliberate recovery choices. Captions explain contact, recording and Retry.
  • Choose reduced motion. Enable it in Preferences: bursts become a single static flash and the camera stays still, while consequences stay readable.

Saves from earlier demos

Straight movement changes the battle rules, so match saves and recordings made with demo.11 or earlier are refused rather than replayed with different movement. Start a new match in this build. The archived 0.8.x player keeps its own saves.

What changed for delivery

Battle effects reuse GameDev's composed presentation burst, trace and camera-kick recipes, now with authored part meshes for debris; The Keepers keeps only its palettes, part shapes and when each effect plays. Straight routes come from Navigation 0.4.0, which replaces grid staircases with shortcuts verified against the world. The rules, recording and Player packages were requalified natively. Every Player owner plus composition passed the per-target qualification on the exact release source.

Detailed results and limitations are linked from the current handoff.

Visible limitations and next delivery

The HUD still uses the full-width header; a compact, width-aware layout with clearer next-step guidance is the next presentation step. Scorch marks fade and do not yet change terrain permanently. Visual quality over the 0.8.3 demo, complete E1 experience acceptance, physical-device performance, accessibility, human review and stable release obligations remain open. A tester demo does not grant those approvals. Please report what you tried, the visible problem and the build identity from the review page.
