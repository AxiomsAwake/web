The Keepers 0.10.0-demo.18 — Mineral Foundry composable 3D combat and live craters

This internal tester build introduces the Mineral Foundry Basin battlefield with composable multi-part PBR units, directional structural damage, live crater deformation, and volumetric explosions. See CHANGELOG.md for the summary of delivered features and the review and launch page for the published web build.

What to try

  • Launch into Mineral Foundry Basin. Opening the Web build drops directly onto the 90 x 70 m Mineral Foundry arena with elevated perimeter ridges, sloped central basin ramps, and protected relay/core pads.
  • Inspect composable 3D units. Blue and Orange forces deploy assembled 38-part Guardians and 36-part Scouts with ceramic panels, copper rims, stepped lug tires, rocker-beam bogies, scrolling grouser tracks, ventilated barrels, and optical turrets.
  • Target structural parts and observe cascades. Shots resolve against actual facing chassis, turret, wheel, and suspension parts. Losing mounts detaches child parts in structural cascades, broken wheels or tracks slow speed and turn rate, and breaching core hull parts destroys the unit.
  • Carve live craters and watch sloped terrain physics. Heavy impacts and explosions deform the mineral ground with rim uplift, charred soot bowls, and glowing fissures. Units pitch, roll, slow uphill, and route around steep blast craters while detached physical debris shards bounce and settle inside crater floors.
  • Switch battlefields or continue matches. Append ?battlefield=breakwater or use Menu -> Opening / Battle setup inside Studio to switch between Mineral Foundry Basin, Breakwater Intake, Headland, and Saddle. Saved matches and recordings preserve live crater deformations deterministically.

Saves and limits

This preview advances the game and component closure (player 0.22.4). Preserve older save files when testing new builds. Stable 0.10.0 acceptance gates — physical-device performance, accessibility, listened audio, and human acceptance — remain open.
