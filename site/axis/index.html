How 3D tic-tac-toe works

Place 3 marks on any straight, contiguous line. A line may stay inside one board or travel through the stack.

—
Layer line Any row, column, or diagonal inside one layer.
↕
Pillar The same square across consecutive layers.
╱
Face diagonal A straight diagonal across a vertical or horizontal plane.
⌁
Space diagonal A diagonal changing row, column, and layer together.

Connect-length matters: on a 4×4×4 board with connect 3, every contiguous three-cell segment is a valid win—not only full edge-to-edge lines.
