Fox

Model: PixelMannen; rigging and animation: tomkranis; glTF conversion: @AsoboStudio and @scurest. The model is CC0-1.0; rigging, animation and conversion are CC-BY-4.0. AxiomsAwake contributors (2026) refined the wildlife mesh with constrained subdivision and smooth normals while retaining its original rig, clips and embedded markings texture. An external fur albedo was edited from the original atlas using the built-in image_gen tool. See the retained license source, attribution metadata, mesh derivation and fur derivation and prompt.
