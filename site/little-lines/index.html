Data, accuracy & offline snapshots

Roads, footprints, mapped stops, crossing positions and bus-route relations come from OSM. Missing building heights, road widths, facade colors and signal timing are estimates. Live GPS mode shows observations only. Simulation uses planned bus stop times with inferred motion; its background cars, pedestrians and signals are synthetic. Disconnected route sections are never joined across buildings. No bus routes in the data means no claimed real bus routes.

Fleet models are stylized, historically documented operator examples, not verified assignments to a particular route. Unknown operators use clearly labeled generic models. See Fleet & field notes for sources.

Requests are manual, cached and rate-limited. Public services can be unavailable or block local-file requests. Change the endpoint or open a saved snapshot. No public Nominatim endpoint is used. For high-volume deployment, use your own or a commercial data service.
