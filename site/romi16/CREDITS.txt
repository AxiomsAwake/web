Romi16 — authorship and restoration credits
Attribution corrected 21 September 2026 from Michael K's first-hand account.

Original game (2001): Michael K

Michael K designed and programmed Romi16 over two months as a gift for his
cousin Romi K's 16th birthday. His original contributions include the world 3D
model (maze and upper level), lava, skydome and map textures, texts, writing/script,
and main source code, excluding third-party libraries.
Copyright (c) 2001 Michael K applies to his original contributions.

Michael reports that he probably created the switch textures, but does not
remember for certain. Their individual authorship remains unconfirmed.

Legacy third-party assets

The 2001 game also used third-party resources and libraries. Most remaining
assets, including all tiling textures (marble, metal, etc.), sounds and music,
were adopted from those resources. The lamp-on image was modified from an
existing third-party lamp-off image. These assets are not attributed to Michael
as their original creator; copyright remains with their respective holders.
Their exact sources, authors and applicable permissions have not yet been
established in this restoration's records. See ASSET_PROVENANCE.txt.

Conversion direction, verification guidance, and publication guidance: Romi K

New WebGL implementation: generated by OpenAI Codex through machine AI
translation and adaptation of the original implementation and supplied materials,
under Romi K's guidance. This contribution credit does not transfer rights or
imply AI ownership. Repository accounts and Git metadata record administration
and changes, not authorship of the underlying assets.

Modern runtime dependencies: three.js and webaudio-tinysynth. Their separate
notices and license texts appear in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt. Those notices cover
the modern libraries, not the unrelated legacy textures, sounds or music.

Optional refreshed marble surfaces (2026): generated using OpenAI Codex's
image-generation tool with the Marble1 and Marble2 textures supplied with the
2001 game as references, under Romi K's direction. These are modern derivative
images. The reference marble textures are reported third-party material, not
Michael's original artwork; generation does not establish clearance of those
references. Original surfaces remain the default.

See LICENSE for the compiled game's free-play terms and the third-party scope
qualification. This credit correction is not evidence of new asset permissions.
