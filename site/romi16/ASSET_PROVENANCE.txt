Romi16 — legacy asset provenance status
Updated 21 September 2026

Evidence: Michael K's first-hand correction, 7 September 2026:
https://github.com/AxiomsAwake/Romi16-play/issues/1

This record distinguishes use in the original game from original authorship.
It is an attribution record, not proof of redistribution clearance. Exact
legacy authors, source packages and permissions remain unresolved where noted.
No public-domain, royalty-free or Creative Commons status is inferred.

Reported original contributions by Michael K
- World 3D model: maze and upper level (converted at runtime to world.json.gz).
- Lava, skydome and map textures: textures/lava.png, textures/Sky.jpg,
  textures/Map.jpg are the corresponding browser-runtime representations.
- Texts, writing/script and main source code, excluding third-party libraries.

Probable but unconfirmed authorship
- textures/SwitchON.jpg and textures/SwitchOFF.jpg: Michael believes he probably
  created the switch textures but does not remember for certain.

Reported third-party material
- All tiling textures, including marble and metal, were adopted from resources
  available in 2001. Exact per-file authors/source packages remain unresolved.
- All original sounds (sounds/*.wav) and music (music/*.mid) were adopted from
  third-party resources. Exact authors/source packages/permissions unresolved.
- textures/lamp-off.png represents a third-party image; the lamp-on image was
  modified from it. Original-source identity and applicable permissions unresolved.
- Other legacy images must not be assigned an individual author merely from a
  filename or presence in the original archive. Per-file provenance unresolved.

Modern derivatives
- refreshed/Marble1.webp and refreshed/Marble2.webp were generated in 2026 using
  the original-game marble textures as references. Those references are reported
  third-party material. Generation and re-encoding do not establish their rights.

Separate modern dependencies
- three.js and webaudio-tinysynth retain their own notices and licenses in
  THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt. Those notices are not licenses for the legacy media.

Limits and follow-through
Michael reports that the 2001 game was personal, non-commercial and not publicly
distributed. That history and the later free-play notice do not establish the
missing legacy permissions. Continue asset-by-asset provenance work: identify
source packages/authors, preserve actual notices or permissions, and distinguish
confirmed evidence from guesses. Unresolved material requires an explicit
rights/retention decision; this record does not silently remove or replace the
original game, claim new permissions, or certify a completed rights audit.

The current browser release is published only through AxiomsAwake/web/romi16/.
Romi16-play is retained as the source of the historical issue, not a publishing
destination. CREDITS.txt and LICENSE state the corrected contribution boundaries.
